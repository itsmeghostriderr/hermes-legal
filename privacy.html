Hermes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Information We Collect

Hermes may receive information from Discord that is necessary to provide its functionality, such as Discord user IDs, usernames, server information, and message content when permitted by Discord.

How We Use Information

Information received by Hermes is used to provide, operate, and improve the application's Discord features and automated responses.

Data Storage

Hermes does not intentionally permanently store personal information unless required for a specific feature of the application.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by third-party services when necessary to provide Hermes functionality.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Hermes. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Hermes developer through the contact information associated with the application.
